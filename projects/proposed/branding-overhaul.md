# Branding Overhaul

## Known Issues

- Older components - supernotify, autoarm, updates2mqtt - lack product specific logos, and only have the generic rhizomatics particle cloud.


## Goals

All products have a logo that clearly conveys the purpose of the product, and has a product line / organizational alignment. 

All will also have recognizable Rhizomatics branding although this is secondary to the product's own presence.

Logos can be tied back from product to organization subtly, by colour choice and common style rather than overt references to the name or organization logo.
