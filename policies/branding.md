# Branding

## Logos

Offer light-mode and dark-mode, and basic (256x256) and high-res (512x512) aka hdpi
The dark mode logos are the default.

Follow Platform branding rules

- https://contributors.home-assistant.io/core/integrations/brands
- https://signalk.org/signalk-plugin-registry/guide.html

Organization level logos are at `branding/organization`. An original higher resolution master copy at `branding/masters`

Application logos should have a consistent theme and colour set, drawing on similar colours from the dark mode icon. Exemplars for this are the `camera_autonotify`,`appliance_autonotify` and `signalk-bluetti-plugin` repos


## Technical

All websites will have:

- Sitemap
- LLMs text, basic and full
- FavIcon
- Apple Touch Icon
- robots.txt, allowing search
- 'Managed by HQ' badge with link to hq.rhizomatics.org.uk
